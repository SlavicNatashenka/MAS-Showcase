# MAS-Showcase
Show Monika any image, including you or other peoples art, photographs, or any image

A cozy submod for Monika After Story that lets you show Monika images you made, someone else made, or found, and save your favorites into your and Monika's very own album.

Features ⸜(｡˃ ᵕ ˂ )⸝♡
- Any .png Filename: Drop any .png into game/Submods/Showcase/Art/ - no more numbering (01.png, 02.png) required. Monika automatically detects new images in the order they were added, it does not matter the name of the image.
- 3 Showcase Options: Choose what you're showing her:
Something I made
Something someone else made
Something I found
- Multi-Image Loop: Dropped multiple images at once? Monika will offer to look at the next one right away until you're caught up.
- Tell Monika "You can keep this one!" to save a copy to your album, then browse anytime with Prev, Next , Fullscreen, and Remove.
- Theme-Matched UI: Follows your MAS theme in both Light Mode and Dark Mode
- Save-Safe: Stored in MAS persistent data and kept/, so updating won't wipe your album.

Extract the Showcase folder and put the game folder into your main MAS directory
