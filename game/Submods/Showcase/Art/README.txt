Showcase — player art instructions
==================================

Put your PNG images in this folder:

  game/Submods/Showcase/Art/

Use ANY filename you want (as long as it ends in .png):

  monika_sketch.png
  cozy_doodle.png
  01.png
  ...

Rules:
- Prefer real PNG files (JPG also often works if renamed to .png — real PNG is safest).
- Any filename works! Monika detects new PNGs automatically and shows them in the order they were added.
- Your album is saved in persistent data — updating/reinstalling Showcase keeps your kept art.
- The album/UI uses normal MAS button styles, so Comfy UI / dark mode apply automatically.

In game:
1. Hey Monika → Misc → "I brought something to show you!"
2. Pick whether it's something you made, something someone else made, or something you found.
3. Look at it (Fullscreen optional) while Monika reacts.
4. Choose "You can keep this one!" to add it to the pastel album
   — or "Just wanted to show you" to move on without keeping it.
5. Later: Misc → "Can I see our album?"

Monika shows new files in the order they were added to this folder.
Each file is only introduced once; keeping is what adds it to the album.
